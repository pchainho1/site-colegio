Services that complement our children's daily lives

Eating

Meals are prepared at the school by a team of experienced cooks, thus ensuring a balanced and adequate diet for the nutritional needs of our children.

Transport

The transport of our children from home to Parque do Falcão and from Parque do Falcão to home is carried out by two employees (a driver and an educational assistant) always safely.

Falcão Digital

Falcão Digital is the virtual distance learning classroom, offered by Colégio Parque do Falcão, in order to shorten the physical distance between students and teachers.

Speech therapy

Communicating is essential!
We developed speech therapy support together with a speech therapist from the PROComSom office.

Special education

Through a special education teacher at Clínica da Educação, we support children who need extra attention.

The curricular activities offered by Colégio Parque do Falcão

English

English classes taught at the college include preparation for Cambridge University certification.

Road Circuit

Students learn to ride a tricycle, developing motor coordination, and learn some basic rules of road traffic in order to become safer pedestrians and cyclists.

Pedagogical Farm

The children play in the land, sow, plant, water and weed, they see what they sow and sow grow and reap.

Playing with Philosophy

Children learn to identify their emotions and ideas and train their ability to express them. They learn to listen carefully to others and realize that different people have different ideas and see the world differently too.

Physical-Motor Expression

In addition to contributing to their physical, emotional and psychological well-being, physical activity produces benefits in the short and medium term, promoting the psycho-motor, cognitive and socio-affective development of the child.

Music

In music classes, children actively listen to a diverse musical repertoire, sing, move and dance to the music, explore the sounds of nature and experiment with percussion musical instruments.

And the Extracurricular Activities

Creative Dance

It introduces the child to the world of dance, through movement games and lots of fun.

Ballet

Ballet allows children to be introduced to the world of dance, through movement games and lots of fun.

Yoga

The practice of Yoga has benefits for physical and mental health, promotes children's well-being and self-esteem, helping them to perceive the world in a more positive way.

Swimming

Swimming is known as a complete sport with many advantages in child development. It's also a great option because it doesn't have major impacts, allowing the child to play more freely.

Mini-tennis

It is excellent for developing hand-eye coordination, gross motor control, fine motor control, balance and co-ordination, while helping in the process of building acceleration, speed, lower limb strength, agility and flexibility.

Karate

Karate Classes, in addition to bringing many benefits from a sporting point of view, also improve the child's behavior, making them more sociable and friendly.

Piano and violin

As the child develops his skills on the piano or violin, the concentration power to perform increasingly complex movements also increases.

Guitar

Learning the acoustic guitar is one of the musical instruments that provides the most satisfaction in children, increasing their self-confidence, improving their creativity and concentration, helping to reduce stress.
