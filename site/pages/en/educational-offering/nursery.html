Nursery

The Educational Services of Creche Parque do Falcão value the individual needs, affection, care and attention dedicated to each of our children.

We create activities that allow cognitive, psycho-motor, personal and social development and creative thinking, combining interaction between the adult and the child.

There is a daily routine where all these areas of development are worked on.

We also pay special attention to care for food, hygiene and rest, with the aim of contributing to the well-being of each child.

Nursery Facilities

The nursery is intended for children up to the age of walking and includes a crib room for the children to rest and a park-room equipped with teaching materials and toys, for the babies' periods of activity.

Educational Program

The 1 Year old Room has a flexibly organized activity room, equipped with play equipment and suited to the needs, interests and games of children between 12 and 24 months.

Educational Program
Food Menu

The 2 Years Old Room is an activity room organized into different exploration areas, equipped with play equipment, suited to the needs, interests and games of children between 24 months and 36 months.

Educational Program
Food Menu

Wooded and grassed area with children's equipment suitable for the psycho-motor development of the little ones. In addition to enabling the development of outdoor physical activity, this space provides excellent moments of play.

Ludic-pedagogical space that intends to be an alternative to the activity room, with different exploration and play areas, which allow increasing the diversity of stimuli and educational experiences for children. The Bebeteca is organized into different areas or corners, such as the psychomotricity and movement area (equipped with psychomotor stimulation materials and music); the storytelling and reading area (with children's books, puppets and theatre); the make-believe area (with costumes, costumes and costume accessories); the construction area (with legos, puzzles, logic and fitting games).

In our Pedagogical Farm we have a pedagogical vegetable garden, where children play in the soil, sow, plant, water, weed, watch it grow and reap what they have sown. We have domestic animals on the farm, which the children visit, caress and feed. This is an excellent space to develop outdoor activities, in contact with the earth and nature.

At Parque do Falcão College Gym, physical-motor expression is developed, with balls, hoops, tunnels, mattresses and other gym equipment suited to the psychomotor development of each age group. In this space the extracurricular activities of ballet, karate and contemporary dance.

The Road Circuit is the space that represents a city, with roads and cycle paths designed and equipped with tricycles and bicycles where children learn to be safe pedestrians and cyclists. This space also offers an excellent opportunity to practice physical activity outdoors, in a playful and fun way.

A spacious, bright and airy space where the pre-school children of Colégio Parque do Falcão have their meals and which offers an excellent alternative to the activities room, for artistic expression and cooking activities.
