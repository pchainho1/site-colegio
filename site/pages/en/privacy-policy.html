Privacy Policy

Pursuant to the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR):

  1. The controller for the processing of personal data is Colégio Parque do Falcão, which can be contacted through the postal address Rua Júlio Augusto Henriques 7, 2840-212, Arrentela, Seixal, or alternatively by telephone +351 212 275 035 and mailing address electronic  geral@colegio-falcao.com 
  1. The Data Protection Officer (DPO) can be contacted through the postal address of the Data Controller or alternatively through the email dpo@colegio-falcao.com
  1. Without prejudice to legal provisions or regulations to the contrary, namely Ordinance No. 1310/2005 of December 21, Personal Data will be kept only for the minimum period necessary for the purposes that motivated its collection or subsequent treatment after which it will be deleted or if it is in the interest of Colégio Parque do Falcão, anonymized in such a way that the holders in question are not or can no longer be identified.
  2. The recipients or categories of recipients of your Personal Data are the general directors of Colégio Parque do Falcão, the pedagogical director, the educators, the early intervention team (name), the teachers responsible for curricular/extracurricular activities, the administrative services of the Colégio Parque do Falcão, Insurance Companies, the Google Suite for Education digital platform, Social Security and the Ministry of Education.
  3. The processing of Personal Data does not involve automated decision-making, including profiling.
  4. Comparisons, interconnections or any other way of interrelating the registered information are not carried out
  5. The Data Subject, represented by the minor's Parent, has the right to file a complaint with a European Union Control Authority regarding the protection of their Personal Data. Colégio Parque do Falcão will cooperate with the Control Authority by providing it with all the information requested by the latter, in the exercise of its powers
  6. The Data Controller applies appropriate technical and organizational measures to ensure a level of security at risk, in order to maintain the integrity and confidentiality of the Personal Data stored or transmitted, as well as the security of the related services offered or accessible through these networks. , systems, policies and procedures
  7. In electronic exchanges of information, because your data may circulate on an open network, they run the risk of being seen and used by unauthorized third parties.
  8. The Holder of Personal Data has the right to withdraw his consent at any time, without compromising the lawfulness of the treatment carried out based on the previously given consent and the right to ask Colégio Parque do Falcão for access to the Personal Data concerning him, as well as its rectification or erasure, and the elimination of the treatment in which it concerns you, or the right to oppose the treatment, as well as the right to data portability
  9. The Personal Data necessary for the performance of the contract in which the Data Subject is a party, for the fulfillment of the legal obligations to which the Data Controller is subject and those that are necessary for the purpose of the legitimate interests pursued by Colégio Parque do Falcão, are not included in the previous point.
  1. Consent is required for further processing of Personal Data for a purpose other than that for which the data was collected. Prior to this treatment, the Controller provides the Data Holder with information on this purpose and any other relevant information.
  2. Colégio Parque do Falcão reserves the right to contact, in case of emergency or in the event of an adverse risk to the rights and freedoms of the minor, the Parental Guardian, the Education Officer or any other related holder with the minor, regardless of their consent.
  3. Although the information provided is generally treated as private at Colégio Parque do Falcão, the data collected will be used in accordance with the Personal Data Protection Regulation, throughout the processing process; however, it may be necessary to transfer Personal Data privately to other bodies, namely Social Security, the Ministry of Education, social workers, doctors, therapists and if the Student is transferred to another Educational Institution.
  4. We rely on the Data Subject's Education Officer to provide us with accurate and complete information and to update the data in relation to any change in the information provided. If you wish to rectify or access your data or the Student's Personal Data, you must send a written communication to the management of Colégio Parque do Falcão, requesting a form for this purpose.
  1. The Guardian informs that he has authorization from the respective holders to contact in case of urgency and/or authorized to collect their Students, in relation to the Personal Data provided to Colégio Parque do Falcão.
  2. Completing or providing the requested data is mandatory, and the lack or inaccuracy of the answers given may compromise the service provided.
  3. Colégio Parque do Falcão keeps photographs of Students at events over the years. It is customary to take pictures of Students during activities in order to create an interesting background of school life. The photographs can be published on the Google Suite for Education platform, and, safeguarding anonymity and the impossibility of identification, can also be used on the Colégio Parque do Falcão website, graphic works related to the presentation of the activities carried out and on social networks.
  4. If the Guardian wishes to remove the image from the various publications, he/she may, at any time, send a written communication to the management of Colégio Parque do Falcão.
  5. If the requests presented by the Data Subject or by the minor's Guardian are manifestly unfounded or excessive, namely due to their repetitive nature, Colégio Parque do Falcão may demand the payment of a reasonable fee taking into account the administrative costs of providing the information or communication, or taking the requested action or refusing to comply with the request.
  6. The purposes of processing the Personal Data provided are:
    • Student Enrollment;
    • Health and Pedagogical Records about the Student, accessible to the technical and auxiliary team;
    • Financial records – accessible to administrative services subcontracted to Colégio Parque do Falcão;
    • Selection of Educators/Teachers and resources for the school;
    • Images – for use as communication material by Colégio Parque do Falcão, accessible on the digital platform (name), presentations at Parent meetings and pedagogical activities;
    • Capture of images by Parents of other Students at Parque do Falcão College events such as: Christmas Party, End of Year Pic Nic, Birthday Parties;
    • Compliance with other legal obligations.
    • Open Day (demonstration of extracurricular activities);
    • Parent meetings with teachers;
    • School events;
    • Notifications about the closure of Colégio Parque do Falcão in case of anomalous situations;
    • Communications regarding the minor's social, emotional and educational progress and to get in touch with the Guardian or whoever he/she appoints in the event of an emergency.
